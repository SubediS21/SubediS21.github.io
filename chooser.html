Chooser

This page generates card sets of different levels using mathematics and JavaScript. Choose a p+1 (prime number + 1) number, or p2 + 1 (power of a prime number + 1) and submit it to see the complete card set of that level and their symbols.

The chooser (containers with cards and symbols) below is interactive. The chooser can help you find specific cards. The unique features of a chooser are:

  • When you click on one card in the card container, all the symbols containing in the card will be highlighted.
  • When you click on two cards in the card container, the common symbol of the two cards will be highlighted
  • Vice versa, when you click on one symbol in the symbol container, all the cards that contains the chosen symbol will be highlighted.
  • When you click on two symbols in the symbol container, the card that contains both of the chosen symbol will be highlighted.
